# Coating

This folder collects discussions, constraints, research briefs, and experiment plans for electrode slurry coating and drying problems in lithium-ion battery manufacturing.

## Working Structure

- `project_constraints_v0.md`: initial project constraints and discussion boundaries.
- `brainstorming/`: divergent mechanism hypotheses and solution ideas.
- `research_briefs/`: prompts and source-backed briefs for deeper research.
- `experiments/`: practical test plans, DOE tables, and validation notes.
- `findings/`: converged conclusions and decision records.

## Current Focus

The project focuses on coating and drying of battery electrode slurry, with attention to manufacturability, realistic test resources, process constraints, and cross-disciplinary mechanisms from fluid mechanics, porous media, polymer physics, suspension rheology, drying science, and related industries.

Current research deliverables are indexed in [`findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/README.md`](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/README.md). New readers should start with the physical-process learning guide, then the deep battery-electrode chapter and the baking analogy chapter. The latest additions include the Dawson thesis deep read, its [complete Chapter 5 Chinese translation](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/21_dawson_thesis_chapter5_full_translation_zh.md), the companion [fixed-thickness cracking-reduction parameter map](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/22_fixed_thickness_cracking_reduction_parameter_map.md), the complete formula/symbol guide, the papers_Yang reading notes, and the one-dimensional thickness-stress mechanism demonstrator. The source PDFs in the local `findings/papers_Yang/` corpus are intentionally excluded from GitHub; page-level local links remain provenance pointers for users who have that corpus. Kumberg 2019 has a separate [full Chinese translation](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/16_kumberg_2019_full_translation_zh.md), [terminology guide](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/17_kumberg_2019_terminology.md), and [evidence-audited deep read](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/18_kumberg_2019_deep_read.md). The [Schabel-to-cracking evidence map](findings/lfp_pvdf_nmp_drying/19_schabel_thick_coating_cracking_map.md) separates direct cracking evidence from precursor-state, mitigation, sensing, and scale-up studies.
